About Me

I'm a Computer Information Systems student at Mount Royal University with a focus on applied AI. I like taking a project from a rough idea to something people can actually use — most recently as the AI developer behind Infinimatch, a media recommendation app, and as co-founder of Red Mesa Development, where I help small businesses build and automate the software they need.

Outside of client work, I build for hackathons and side projects when I get the chance — Planty, a habit-tracking robotic plant, took 1st place at MRU Hacks 2025. I'm always looking for the next problem worth building a solution for, whether that's applying machine learning, automating a clunky process, or just making something fun.
